A complete sponsoring/ads plan to grow the account, increase reach, and convert followers into clients (design / creative services).

## 2. Define the Goal of the Ad

Instagram (Meta Ads Manager) lets you choose an objective. Pick **one per campaign**:

| Goal | Objective to choose | Best when |
|---|---|---|
| Get more followers | **Profile visits** / Engagement | Account < 2k followers |
| Get DMs / leads | **Messages** (Click-to-DM) | You sell a service |
| Get website clicks | **Traffic** | You have a portfolio site |
| Get sales | **Conversions** | You have a shop / pixel set up |

For `@saifamourii` (creative / design service) → **start with "Messages" (Click-to-DM)** + a secondary "Profile visits" campaign for branding.

---

## 3. Audience Targeting

Create **3 audiences** and split-test them:

**Audience A — Local clients (best ROI for freelance designers)**
- Location: your city + 30 km radius (or Tunisia / France / wherever your clients are)
- Age: 25–55
- Interests: Small business owners, Entrepreneurship, Restaurants, E-commerce, Shopify, Real estate
- Languages: French + Arabic (or your market's language)

**Audience B — Creative niche / inspiration**
- Age: 18–34
- Interests: Behance, Dribbble, Adobe Photoshop, Adobe Illustrator, Graphic design, Typography
- Use this for *follower growth*, not conversions.

**Audience C — Lookalike (after 30+ DMs received)**
- Build a Custom Audience from people who messaged you
- Then create a 1% Lookalike → this becomes your top performer over time.

## 4. Budget & Bidding

**Starter budget recommendation:**
- **5 €/day per ad set** × 3 ad sets = **15 €/day** for the first 7 days.
- After 7 days: kill the 2 worst performers, scale the winner to 20–30 €/day.
- Total first-month test budget: **~300–400 €**.

**Bidding:** Start with *Highest volume* (automatic). Switch to *Cost cap* only after you know your average cost per result.

## 5. Creative — What the Ad Should Look Like

**Format priority (2026):**
1. **Reels Ads** (9:16, 7–15 sec) — cheapest reach right now.
2. **Carousel Ads** (1:1, 4–6 slides) — best for explaining a service.
3. **Single image** — only as a retargeting reminder.

**Reel ad script template (15 sec) — for a designer:**
```
0–2s   HOOK: "Ton logo te fait perdre des clients."
2–6s   PROBLEM: show 3 ugly DIY logos
6–11s  SOLUTION: timelapse of you redesigning one
11–13s PROOF: "+47 marques rebrandées en 2026"
13–15s CTA: "DM 'LOGO' pour un devis gratuit"
```

**Carousel ad template (6 slides):**
1. Hook headline ("3 erreurs qui tuent ton branding")
2–4. The 3 mistakes with visuals
5. Before/after of your work
6. CTA: "Envoie-moi 'BRAND' en DM"

**Rules that always work:**
- Subtitles burned into the video (85% watch muted).
- Face on camera in the first 2 seconds = +30% retention.
- Show the *result*, not the process.
- One clear CTA, repeated twice.

## 6. Boost Post vs Ads Manager

| | Boost Post (the blue button) | Ads Manager (business.facebook.com) |
|---|---|---|
| Setup speed | 30 seconds | 10 minutes |
| Targeting depth | Basic | Full (lookalikes, retargeting, placements) |
| Cost per result | 2–4× more expensive | Optimized |
| Recommended? | Only for awareness of an already-viral post | **Yes — use this for everything that matters** |

→ Use **Ads Manager**, not the in-app boost button.

## 7. 30-Day Sponsoring Plan

**Week 1 — Test**
- Launch 3 ad sets (audiences A/B/C) × 1 creative each = 3 ads.
- Objective: Messages.
- Budget: 5 €/day each.
- Don't touch anything for 5 days (let the algorithm learn).

**Week 2 — Cut & double down**
- Kill ads with CPM > 15 € or CTR < 1%.
- Increase winner's budget by +20% / day max (avoid resetting learning phase).

**Week 3 — Add creative variants**
- Take the winning audience, launch 3 NEW creatives against it.
- This is how you scale without audience fatigue.

**Week 4 — Retarget**
- Build a Custom Audience: "everyone who watched 50%+ of my video ads in the last 14 days".
- Show them a testimonial reel + offer ("-20% ce mois-ci").
- Retargeting CPM is usually 3–5× cheaper.

---

## 8. KPIs to Track (weekly)

| Metric | Healthy benchmark |
|---|---|
| CPM (cost per 1000 impressions) | 3–8 € |
| CTR (link click rate) | > 1.5% |
| Cost per DM / lead | 1–4 € |
| Cost per follower | 0.10–0.30 € |
| Profile visit → follow rate | > 15% |

If a number is off → fix the matching layer:
- Bad CPM → audience too narrow or creative weak.
- Good CTR but no follows → profile/bio is the problem, not the ad.
- Lots of DMs but no clients → pricing/offer problem, not ads.

## 9. Organic Content That Makes Ads Cheaper

Meta rewards accounts that post consistently — your CPM drops when your organic engagement is high.

- 3 Reels / week (one of them = your ad creative, posted organically first).
- 2 Carousels / week (educational: "5 fonts I use", "before/after").
- Daily story (poll, question sticker, BTS).
- Reply to every DM within 1 hour during ad campaigns.

## 10. Quick Wins Specific to @saifamourii

Based on the account being a personal-brand designer page:

1. **Run a "free logo audit" lead magnet ad** → people DM "AUDIT", you record a 60-sec Loom critiquing their logo, then upsell. Cost per qualified lead: usually < 2 €.
2. **Before/After reel ads** outperform everything else for designers — they're visual, fast, and proof-driven.
3. **Geo-target competitors' clients**: target "Small business owners" in your city who follow other agencies.
4. **Testimonial carousel ads** in week 3+ — social proof > self-promotion.
5. **Always offer a tangible next step in the DM**: a Calendly link, a price list PDF, or a portfolio.
